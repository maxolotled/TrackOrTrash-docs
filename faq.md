# Frequently Asked Questions
::: details What is Track or Trash?
Track or Trash is the easiest way to sort your Spotify playlists in 2026! Simply use arrow keys or the buttons to sort your songs into the right category: track, trash, or love.
:::
::: details I can't sign in!
Oh no! Make sure you followed the exact instructions in the [tutorial](/spotify). Your client ID, secret, and redirect URI must be configured **exactly** as shown in the tutorial, or it will not work! Also refer to the error message given by Spotify.
:::
::: details Is Track or Trash secure?
Yes! Our entire app is open-source on [github](https://github.com/maxolotled/TrackorTrash), and all information you provide is stored on your local device and doesn't get sent to anyone except for spotify.
:::
::: details I got a timeout-error!
No problem! Just refresh the page and sign in again - your progress is saved.
:::
::: details I got an error that isn't described here, or I need to reach out to you for a different reason!
Oh no! Please let me know via [github](https://github.com/maxolotled/trackortrash) or [email](mailto:max@trackortrash.me).
:::
